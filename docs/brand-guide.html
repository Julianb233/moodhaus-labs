Logo

The stacked lockup

MOODHAUS over the three-word promise over LABS, closed by a short hairline. Set in Montserrat Light, centered, with generous tracking. Keep at least the height of the letter “O” clear on every side.

Monogram

For favicons, social avatars and stamps where the full lockup would be too small.

Minimum size

Stacked lockup: 30 mm / 140 px wide (the tagline line becomes illegible below that). Under that, use the horizontal wordmark or monogram.

Don’t

Don’t condense or remove the tracking, set it in bold, recolor it outside the palette, put it on a busy photo without a forest scrim, or add effects such as shadows or gradients.

04
Graphic motif

Contour lines

Topographic contours are the brand’s signature: they speak to landscape, grading and site, the starting point of every Moodhaus project. Use them as a frame around content (heavier at the edges, open in the center), never as a busy all-over pattern behind text.

Specs

Stroke 1–1.5 px (0.25 pt in print), champagne on forest at 45–80% opacity, or bronze on paper. A secondary curved bronze line (the sweep on the back of the card) can be used alone on light layouts.

Calm, assured, personal

07
Photography and motion

Golden hour, coastline, quiet

Imagery should feel like an architectural magazine shot at the end of the day in coastal San Diego.

Subjects

Finished homes at dusk with interior light on, still water and reflecting pools, native and Mediterranean planting, stone, timber, the Pacific horizon. People appear rarely and candidly.

Treatment

Warm, low-contrast grade; shadows lean green, highlights lean gold. Frame images in arches (a nod to the logo’s window) or clean rectangles with 4–24 px radius. Use a forest scrim before placing type on photos.

Motion

Slow and cinematic: drone push-ins, parallax, lines that draw themselves. Nothing bounces. Always honor reduced-motion settings.
